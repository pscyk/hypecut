- **Inter** and **IBM Plex Mono**, distributed by Fontsource under their bundled

- The product film's synth soundtrack is generated by `motion/make_audio.py`
  without third-party music or sound-effect recordings. Its demonstration
  video excerpts are the same attributed source material described above.
